Charlie Kirk Memorial Statue Vandalized in Phoenix

A recently unveiled memorial statue of Charlie Kirk outside Turning Point USA's Phoenix headquarters was defaced with red paint overnight, and police are investigating.

By Rally Point News Desk · Published September 13, 2026 · Multi-source verified synthesis

Reuters reported Sunday that the statue was vandalized overnight outside Turning Point USA's headquarters in Phoenix. Photographs and reporting from multiple outlets show red paint on the memorial, while a nearby sign was also defaced. Phoenix police are investigating the incident and have asked anyone with information to contact authorities.

What is established

The eight-foot memorial statue was unveiled on September 10, the first anniversary of Kirk's killing at Utah Valley University. Local FOX 10 reporting from the unveiling said Turning Point USA commissioned the statue and installed it outside the organization's Phoenix headquarters.

Reuters and the New York Post reported that the statue was found vandalized Sunday morning. Red paint had been applied to several parts of the statue, and a nearby memorial sign was also marked. Turning Point USA publicly condemned the vandalism.

What remains uncertain

No suspect has been publicly identified, and authorities have not announced a motive. The available reporting does not establish who carried out the vandalism or whether it was connected to any organization or broader political activity. Those questions remain part of the police investigation.

Why it matters

The incident comes only days after the memorial was unveiled and one year after Kirk's death, giving the vandalism heightened political and symbolic attention. Kirk founded Turning Point USA and became a prominent conservative activist, making the statue both a memorial and a political symbol. The most important unresolved facts are straightforward: who damaged it, why, and whether police identify any additional circumstances surrounding the incident.

Editorial note: Rally Point News is not attributing motive or responsibility beyond what law enforcement and verified reporting establish. No suspect had been publicly identified at publication time.

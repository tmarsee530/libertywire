Site policy

Privacy

This page explains, in plain language, the main ways Rally Point News and the services it uses may process information when you visit the site or subscribe to The Rally Brief.

Last updated: September 13, 2026.

Rally Point News does not intentionally sell personal information. The site is built as a largely static publication, and subscriber email addresses are not stored in the public website repository.

Site analytics

Rally Point News uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate site usage, such as page views, sessions, device type, approximate geographic region, referral source, and interactions with site features. Google may set or read cookies or similar identifiers as part of that service.

Newsletter subscriptions

The Rally Brief signup form and newsletter delivery are handled by Kit. If you subscribe, information you submit through the form is processed by Kit for subscription and email-delivery purposes. Rally Point News does not publish subscriber addresses in its website repository. Newsletter emails provide an unsubscribe mechanism.

Advertising and third-party services

The site includes Google AdSense account configuration and may display advertising if and when advertising is activated. Advertising and analytics providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. Rally Point also links to third-party news publishers; once you leave Rally Point News, the destination site's policies apply.

Local game data

Rally Point Games may store game progress, completion history, and streak information locally in your browser or device storage so that daily games can remember your results. This local game state is designed to stay on your device unless a feature explicitly says otherwise.

Information you send directly

If you contact Rally Point News directly, the information in your message may be used to respond, evaluate a correction, or review a news tip. Do not send highly sensitive personal information through ordinary email.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as Rally Point News adds or removes services. Material changes should be reflected on this page with a revised update date.

This notice is intended as a straightforward description of current site practices, not as a promise that third-party providers will never change their own systems or policies.

# Flagship timeline experiment — October 2026

The launch set is ten scoped, source-linked event timelines listed at `/stories/#featured`. The homepage links to Featured. Each guide provides a sourced starting point, a reason to follow and specific developments to watch. Current status and reverse chronology remain live; the guide does not predict outcomes.

## Acquisition

`data/distribution_candidates.json` contains ten draft X posts with the `flagship_timeline` campaign tag. Review source accuracy and freshness before sharing. Automatic posting remains disabled. Reader sharing uses `utm_source=reader`, `utm_medium=share`, and the same campaign. These links identify a distribution channel, not a person.

The standard sitemap contains only authoritative canonical timeline URLs. The two reviewed Cornell aliases retain their URLs, point readers to the single maintained event and declare its canonical URL. A withheld contaminated timeline remains accessible with an editorial-review notice and is excluded from discovery. Raw history is retained.

## Measurement

GA4 receives `timeline_open`, `timeline_engaged` (20 seconds of visible reading), `timeline_source_open`, `timeline_share`, and `timeline_end_reached`. These include `timeline_id`, `featured_timeline` and `experiment_id=flagship-timelines-2026-10`. Existing `story_follow`, `timeline_return_visit` and unseen-update events measure retention. Opening the end card is an exposure measure, not proof of comprehension. A share click counts only after native sharing or clipboard copying succeeds.

Use GA4 acquisition reports to compare the campaign with organic-search traffic, then compare engaged timeline readers, follows and returning readers. Exclude owner traffic using the property's existing controls. Do not infer readership from build logs or crawler requests.

Use Search Console's page report to inspect the ten exact canonical URLs and URL Inspection to distinguish discovered, crawled and indexed pages. Check impressions, clicks and search queries at 7, 14 and 28 days. Check sitemap HTTP status and links separately: crawlability does not prove indexing.

Fresh Search Console and GA4 reporting through the current GSC Wizard connection is blocked by its expired subscription. No new purchase was made and no current traffic baseline is claimed. Instrumentation does not by itself create an analytics dashboard or key-event configuration.

## Decision rule

First target: sustained 100 genuine visits/day across the site, with repeat timeline readership. This is a milestone, not a forecast. Evaluate the pilot after 28 days: keep topics and distribution channels that bring engaged readers back; revise weak pages using observed questions and source coverage. Do not manufacture article volume, refresh dates or clicks to meet the target.

## Editorial repairs

`data/timeline_editorial.json` contains reviewed event scopes, canonical aliases, exclusions and limited repeat-fact phases. They operate on publication copies and historical matching candidates; they never erase the archived source reports. Regex scopes constrain membership, not truth. Allegations remain allegations, additional repeat reports do not manufacture independent confirmation, and new evidence requires continued review. Remove or revise dated guides when events materially change.
